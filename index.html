No. The dashboard runs entirely locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

Yes — it was built by a medical student dealing with exactly that kind of workload, and works well for anyone juggling demanding, inconsistent schedules.

No. Human OS is a self-assessment and personal-development framework, not a medical diagnostic device or a substitute for professional medical care.
