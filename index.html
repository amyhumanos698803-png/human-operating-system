What's wrong with me? Which system is signaling?

That single substitution is the mental shift this book calls Diagnostic Mode — and once you see it, you can't unsee it.

A pilot whose fuel light comes on doesn't conclude the plane hates them. They check the gauge it's wired to, and respond to what it's actually reporting. That's not a metaphor. It's a literal, practical habit of mind.

Symptoms are not the problem. Symptoms are information. Don't judge the person — diagnose the system.
